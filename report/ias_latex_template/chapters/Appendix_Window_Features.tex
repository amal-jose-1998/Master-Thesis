\chapter{Complete Window-Level Observation Features}
\label{appendix:window_features}

This appendix lists the complete set of window-level features used to construct the observation vector of the proposed model. 
The features are grouped according to their role in the representation. 

\section{Ego-motion features}

\begin{table}[htbp]
    \centering
    \footnotesize
    \caption{Window-level ego-motion features.}
    \label{tab:appendix_ego_features}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{4.2cm}X@{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Window feature} & \textbf{Aggregation rule} \\
        \midrule
        \texttt{vx\_last} & Most recent longitudinal velocity value in the window \\
        \texttt{vx\_slope} & Temporal slope of longitudinal velocity over the window \\
        \texttt{ax\_last} & Most recent longitudinal acceleration value in the window \\
        \texttt{vy\_last} & Most recent lateral velocity value in the window \\
        \texttt{vy\_slope} & Temporal slope of lateral velocity over the window \\
        \texttt{ay\_last} & Most recent lateral acceleration value in the window \\
        \texttt{ax\_neg\_frac} & Fraction of frames in the window with negative longitudinal acceleration \\
        \texttt{ax\_pos\_frac} & Fraction of frames in the window with positive longitudinal acceleration \\
        \texttt{ax\_zero\_frac} & Fraction of frames in the window with zero longitudinal acceleration \\
        \texttt{ay\_neg\_frac} & Fraction of frames in the window with negative lateral acceleration \\
        \texttt{ay\_pos\_frac} & Fraction of frames in the window with positive lateral acceleration \\
        \texttt{ay\_zero\_frac} & Fraction of frames in the window with zero lateral acceleration \\
        \texttt{jerk\_x\_p95} & 95th percentile of longitudinal jerk within the window \\
        \texttt{jerk\_y\_p95} & 95th percentile of lateral jerk within the window \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
